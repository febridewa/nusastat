NusaStat is an independent statistics portal about Indonesia. Figures come from BPS (Statistics Indonesia), Bank Indonesia, the Ministry of Finance, UNDP and the World Bank, and each figure names its source and year on the page where it appears. NusaStat is not affiliated with BPS or any government body.

Built for the make.fun “best national statistics site” bounty. Data current to 5 November 2026. All figures rounded; download the CSV datasets to check them.
